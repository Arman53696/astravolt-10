PRIVACY POLICY

Astravolt by Nexora Studio — effective 17 September 2026. This policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Contact us any time at support.nexorastudio@gmail.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT

• Guest play — no account needed. Your coins, upgrades, ships and level progress are saved only on your own device (local storage) and never leave it.

• Google sign-in (optional) — if you choose to sign in, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, only to show your profile and keep your save synced.

• Game progress — score, coins, upgrades, unlocked ships and level progress, so your save can be restored and synced between your devices.

• Usage data — anonymous usage and crash statistics collected through Google Analytics for Firebase to fix bugs and improve the game.

• Advertising & device identifiers — the advertising ID (or a resettable device identifier) may be used to show ads and measure ad performance.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Astravolt relies on these trusted providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

• Google AdMob — serves the ads inside the game. AdMob may use your advertising ID to deliver and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Privacy → Ads). You can also remove all ads permanently with the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase.

• Google Firebase — powers secure sign-in, cloud save (stored in the Singapore region) and anonymous usage analytics.

• Google Play Billing — processes all in-app purchases. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card details.

WHAT WE NEVER DO

• We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone except the providers above, strictly to run the game.

• We do not access your contacts, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Guest progress stays on your device until you clear your browser/app data or reset your progress in Settings. Cloud saves are kept while your account is active. To permanently delete your account and cloud save, email us at support.nexorastudio@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

CHILDREN

Astravolt is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

PERMISSIONS

The game uses the Internet connection (ads, cloud save, purchases) and the advertising ID permission. No other sensitive permissions are requested.

CONTACT

Questions about privacy? Email support.nexorastudio@gmail.com — we reply to every message.
